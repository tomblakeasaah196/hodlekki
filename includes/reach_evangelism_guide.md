## Before you go out

- **Pray first.** Evangelism is spiritual work before it is conversation. Ask the Holy Spirit to go ahead of you, open hearts, and give you the right words. *"Not by might nor by power, but by My Spirit," says the Lord of hosts.* (Zechariah 4:6)
- **Go in love, not pressure.** People are won by love long before they are won by argument. *"The love of Christ compels us."* (2 Corinthians 5:14)
- **Go in pairs** and stay together, as Jesus sent the disciples two by two (Luke 10:1).
- **Remember who does the saving.** You plant and water; God gives the increase (1 Corinthians 3:6–7). Your job is to be faithful, not to force a result.

## Starting the conversation

- Smile, greet warmly and introduce yourself by name: *"Hi, I'm ___ from Household of David Lekki. We're out today praying for our neighbours — is there anything we can pray with you about?"*
- **Listen more than you speak.** Ask about their family, their work, what they are trusting God for. Every life has an open door.
- Be respectful of their time, their faith background and their space. Never argue — if they are not ready, bless them and move on.

## Sharing the Gospel simply

1. **God loves you** and made you for relationship with Him. (John 3:16)
2. **Sin separates us from God** — all of us have fallen short. (Romans 3:23; Romans 6:23)
3. **Jesus paid the price.** He died for our sins and rose again, so we can be forgiven and made new. (Romans 5:8; 1 Corinthians 15:3–4)
4. **We receive Him by faith** — turning to God and trusting Jesus as Lord. (Romans 10:9–10; Ephesians 2:8–9)
5. **Invite a response:** *"Would you like to give your life to Jesus today?"* If yes, lead a simple prayer of faith, then celebrate with them — heaven does! (Luke 15:10)

Share your own story too: two minutes on what your life was like, how you met Jesus, and what has changed. A testimony is hard to argue with. (Revelation 12:11)

## Inviting them to church

- Invite them **personally and specifically**: *"We'd love to see you at church this Sunday — can I meet you at the entrance?"*
- Offer to sit with them, or to connect them with someone who lives nearby.
- If they say yes, tick **"Will come to church"** when you log them, so the team can follow up and welcome them.
- Ask for the best time to call, and let them know someone will reach out this week.

## Ministering to different hearts

- **New converts:** assure them of salvation (1 John 5:11–13) and encourage them to read the Gospel of John.
- **Broken or hurting:** listen, pray, and point them to the God who heals the brokenhearted (Psalm 147:3; Matthew 11:28–30).
- **Backslidden or dechurched:** welcome them home without judgement — the Father runs to the returning son (Luke 15:20). Hebrews 10:24–25 is a gentle word about gathering together.
- **Already saved:** encourage them, pray together, and invite them to serve with us.

## Scriptures for your personal time

Soak in these before and after outreach:

- Matthew 28:18–20 — the Great Commission
- Mark 16:15 — go into all the world
- Acts 1:8 — power to be witnesses
- Romans 1:16 — not ashamed of the gospel
- Romans 10:13–15 — how beautiful are the feet
- 2 Corinthians 5:17–21 — ministers of reconciliation
- 1 Peter 3:15 — always ready to give an answer, with gentleness
- Proverbs 11:30 — he who wins souls is wise
- Daniel 12:3 — those who turn many to righteousness shine like stars
- Luke 19:10 — the Son of Man came to seek and save the lost
- John 4:35 — the fields are white for harvest
- Isaiah 6:8 — "Here am I, send me"

## Books to read in your personal time

- **Out of the Saltshaker and into the World** — Rebecca Manley Pippert (evangelism as a natural way of life)
- **Evangelism and the Sovereignty of God** — J. I. Packer (why we share, and who saves)
- **The Soul Winner** — Charles H. Spurgeon (a classic on the heart of a soul winner)
- **Becoming a Contagious Christian** — Bill Hybels & Mark Mittelberg (practical, relational witness)
- **Good News to the Poor** — Tim Chester (the gospel lived in word and deed)
- **Tell the Truth** — Will Metzger (presenting the whole gospel with love)
- **The Master Plan of Evangelism** — Robert E. Coleman (how Jesus made disciples)
