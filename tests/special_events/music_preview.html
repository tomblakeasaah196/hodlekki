Tap anywhere, or scroll.

The page loaded silent, because every browser blocks audible autoplay until you interact with it. Your first tap, scroll or key press fades the track in at 30%. Watch the ring around the speaker in the bottom-left corner — it is driven by the live waveform, so it breathes with the music rather than on a timer.

What to try
  1. Tap the speaker to mute. Reload — it stays muted, because the choice is remembered.
  2. Tap the dial to slide out the volume rail. Drag it to zero: that mutes too, and the two controls agree.
  3. Switch to another browser tab. The music stops, and resumes when you come back.
  4. Turn on your OS "reduce motion" setting and reload: the ring stops moving, the music still works.
  5. Turn your phone to silent. Nothing on this page can detect that — the sound simply stops being audible.
