# How to use Reach

## 1. Overview

Reach is where our evangelism work lives: every street outreach, every person we meet, and every call, message and visit that follows. It has four tabs:

- **Campaigns** — plan outreaches and share their public capture link.
- **Follow-Up** — the queue of everyone we met, who is looking after them, and what has happened since.
- **Analytics** — how the ministry is doing over any date range, plus the monthly PDF.
- **How to Use** — this guide.

Anyone in the Reach department can capture, claim and follow up leads. **HODs, Directors and pastors** can also assign leads to people and see everyone's overdue work.

## 2. Creating a campaign

1. On **Campaigns**, click **New Campaign**.
2. Enter a clear **title** (for example *Ikate Street Evangelism*) and the **location**. Add the event type, date and times if you know them.
3. Optionally upload the campaign **flyer** (JPG, PNG or WebP, up to 5 MB). It becomes the WhatsApp link preview and the background of the capture page. No flyer? The **default campaign image** is used instead.
4. Click **Write with AI** — it writes the WhatsApp description and picks a fitting scripture from the title and location. Not quite right? Click **Another option** to cycle through up to three versions, or edit the text yourself.
5. Choose the **form length** — how much volunteers ask each person:
   - **Quick** — name, phone, where they are spiritually and "open to a visit". About 10 seconds. Best for busy streets.
   - **Standard** — adds area and prayer request.
   - **Detailed** — adds age group, marital status, language, best time to call and notes. Every extra question is optional.

Renaming a campaign changes its link, so share the new one. A campaign with captures cannot be deleted — it is marked *Cancelled* instead so the record stays intact.

**Campaign settings** (the gear button next to New Campaign, for HODs, Directors and pastors):

- **Event types** — add your own (for example *Market Storm*) or remove ones you don't use. Removing a type keeps it on campaigns that already use it.
- **Default campaign image** — upload the picture used when a campaign has no flyer (best at 1200 × 630), or go back to the church photo.

## 3. Sharing the public link

Click **Share** on a campaign card. You get:

- the **public link** (`/reach.php?c=…`) — no login needed;
- a ready-made **WhatsApp message** with the description, scripture and link — tap **Send via WhatsApp**. The preview shows the flyer (or the default image);
- a **QR code** you can print or show on a phone at the meeting point.

Volunteers open the link, enter the phone number they are registered with, confirm their name, and start logging people. Only numbers already in our family database can capture. Voice capture fills the form from one spoken sentence — always check it before saving.

## 4. Working the follow-up queue

The **Follow-Up** tab has five sub-tabs:

- **Not Spoken To** — nobody has reached them yet (a single unanswered attempt still counts here).
- **Spoken To** — at least one follow-up was *Reached*.
- **Cold** — two or more attempts and no contact yet.
- **Converted** — handed over to Embrace.
- **All** — everything, including people who asked not to be contacted.

Use the filters to narrow the list by campaign, category, area, assignee, "willing to visit" or a name/phone search. The red banner at the top counts leads **assigned more than 5 days ago with no follow-up** — tap it to see only those.

**Claiming and assigning**

- See someone **Unassigned**? Tap **Claim** — they are now yours.
- HODs, Directors and pastors open a lead and use **Assign / Reassign** to hand it to a Reach member. The new (and previous) assignee is notified.

**Logging a follow-up** — open the lead, choose the channel and outcome, add a note and, if you agreed on one, a **next touch** date. The lead moves between sub-tabs automatically. Choosing *Requested no contact* stops further follow-up.

The red number next to **Reach** in the sidebar is *unassigned leads + your overdue leads*.

## 5. Pushing to Embrace

When someone is ready to be welcomed as a first timer, open the lead and click **Push to Embrace as 1st Timer**. This:

- creates their profile as a **1st Timer**, with "Invited by: Reach — capturer — campaign";
- copies their phone, address and prayer request;
- notifies Embrace leaders (and IDI, to review the new profile);
- moves the lead to **Converted**.

If their phone number already exists in the family database, Reach links to that profile instead of creating a duplicate. A phone number is required. Only the assignee or a HOD/Director/pastor can push.

## 6. Reading analytics

Pick a period with the chips (**This Month**, **Last Month**, **Last 3 Months**, **Year to Date**) or **Custom** dates.

- **Souls Captured** — people met in the period.
- **Campaigns Run** — non-cancelled campaigns dated in the period.
- **Follow-up Rate** — share of those people with at least one follow-up.
- **Conversion Rate** — share pushed to Embrace.

The funnel shows *Captured → Spoken To → Visited / Converted*. The donut splits people by spiritual category, the area chart shows the top 15 neighbourhoods, and the tables rank volunteers (guests included) and compare campaigns. **Export CSV** downloads every lead in the period for a spreadsheet.

## 7. Generating the monthly PDF

Choose the period, then click **Generate PDF**. The two-page report has the KPIs, funnel, campaigns, top capturers, categories, areas, volunteer activity, a short written summary of the month, recent prayer requests (first names only) and testimonies.

Testimonies only appear for converted leads where a HOD has ticked **Share testimony in report** in the lead drawer — always ask the person first.

## 8. FAQ

**A volunteer says their number "isn't in our family database".**
They need to be added as a member first (IDI / Congregation). Check the number they are typing matches their profile.

**The same person was captured twice.**
Reach merges captures with the same phone in the same campaign automatically and shows every capturer in the lead's *Capture chain*.

**Why is a lead still in Not Spoken To after I logged a call?**
One unanswered attempt isn't "Cold" yet. Log the next attempt — after two with no contact it moves to Cold; any *Reached* moves it to Spoken To.

**Can I undo a push to Embrace?**
No — the profile now belongs to Embrace. Ask an Embrace leader to update it.

**Who can see this module?**
Super Admins, pastors, and members of the Reach / Evangelism and IDI departments.
