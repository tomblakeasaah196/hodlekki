# Mail: how to use it

## For everyone who works from a mailbox

- **Mail** is in the menu once you work from a mailbox: your own (`firstname.lastname@hodlekki.org`), your
  department's, or a church-wide one such as embrace@ or info@. The number next to it is how many conversations
  in your inboxes are unread.
- Pick a mailbox at the top when you have more than one. **Check mail** (the circling arrows) reads the server straight away;
  otherwise new mail shows within a minute.
- Folders are on the left (on a phone, pick one above the list). Click a conversation to read it; on a shared
  mailbox, opening it marks it read for the whole team, and it says who opened it.
- **Search** looks through the last 12 months; `from:ada` looks at the sender only. For older mail, use
  **Search server** or **Load older** at the bottom of the list.
- Pictures from the internet stay hidden until you choose **Show pictures** (once) or **Always show** (that
  sender, for you only).
- **Archive**, **Spam**, **Trash** and **Move to…** are on top of a conversation; a message after each one offers
  **Undo**. Press **?** for the keyboard shortcuts.
- Out-of-office answers are marked **Automatic reply**.
- **New message**, and on a conversation **Reply**, **Reply all** or **Forward**, open the composer. It saves as you
  type; close it and the draft waits in **Drafts** and on the **Outbox** page (the clock button).
- After **Send** you have 10 seconds to **Undo**. The arrow beside Send schedules it (tomorrow or next Monday at
  08:00, or a time you pick).
- Files: **Attach**, or drop them on the message; 18 MB together. Send to 50 people at most; use Campaigns for more.
- Your signature is added when the message leaves: full on new mail, short on replies. Change your numbers on
  **My signature** (link in the composer).
- If a message does not go, the bell tells you; open it from the Outbox to fix it or send it again.
- **Shared mailboxes**: above a conversation, set **Open**, **Waiting** or **Done** and choose who it is assigned to
  (HODs and others with *assign*). It goes to Waiting by itself when a reply leaves, and back to Open when they
  write again. You see who else is reading or replying; if a teammate replied while you were writing, Send asks first.
- **Alerts**: **My mail settings** (the sliders button) chooses pushes per mailbox (all, assigned to me, off), the
  daily summary, and which key alerts go to your personal email (sender and subject only).
- **AI** (only on mailboxes where it is switched on): **Summarize** above a conversation, and **AI draft** in the
  composer. Read and change the draft before you send: AI never sends anything.
- There is no password to remember or type: the church's mail is read in the app only.

## For mail admins (the Mailboxes screen)

Open **Mail**, then **Mailboxes**.

- **The numbers at the top**: how many of hodlekki.org's 15 mailboxes are used, how many are kept for personal
  mailboxes, and the hosting account's total across all its sites.
- **New mailbox**: a church-wide or department mailbox. Choose the address and the name people see in the From
  line. It appears within a minute ("waiting for the server").
- **Issue a personal mailbox**: type two letters of the person's name. Only people whose role or seat allows a
  personal mailbox can be chosen (Roles & permissions decides that). The address is suggested from their name.
- **On the server, not in the ERP**: mailboxes made in cPanel. **Bring in** gives them a new password; anything
  signed in with the old one stops. system@ and dmarc@ always stay outside.
- **Who works from it** (the … menu on a row): add lines for a role, a seat in a department (for example the HOD and
  Deputy HOD of Embrace), everyone in a department, or one person, each at a level: *Read*, *Read and send*,
  *Read, send and assign*, *Manage*. Mail admins do not read a mailbox unless a line gives them access.
- **Reset password**, **Suspend** / **Resume**, **Storage limit**, **Automatic reply**, **Remove mailbox** (type the
  address to confirm: its mail is deleted on the server).
- **AI in Mail** (Health tab): once the owner confirms the Gemini plan is paid, **Record paid plan**, then **Turn on**
  per mailbox (never personal or pastoral ones). People also need *AI summaries and draft replies in Mail* in
  Roles & permissions.
- **Activity**: every action and whether the server did it. **Health**: what the system checks, and the number of
  slots kept for personal mailboxes.

## When someone with a personal mailbox leaves

The app notices overnight (their role or seat no longer allows a personal mailbox, or their account was suspended).
Their mailbox is closed at once, an automatic reply asks senders to write to info@ for 30 days, and then the mailbox
is removed, with a forwarder to info@.

Nobody reads a departed person's mail, except through **Ask to export its mail** (Mailboxes, the … menu). The
request needs two approvals from two different people: one with the admin approval and one with the pastoral
approval (the Resident Pastor). Then the person who asked can download it for 7 days. Every step is recorded.
