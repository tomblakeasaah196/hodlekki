# How to use Charis

Charis is the welfare desk: it marks birthdays and life events, follows up on members who stop coming, plans event tasks, tracks the welfare budget and runs the library desk.

## The new look

- **Tabs** across the top: Welfare, Events, Finance, Library and this Guide. The link remembers your tab.
- **Tiles** on the Welfare tab show the counts. Tap Birthdays or Life events to open the list; tap AWOL to jump to the case list.
- **The (i) icons** explain a section or a field. Hover, focus or tap them.
- **Phones first**: every list becomes cards on a small screen, and every form opens as a sheet.

## Welfare: AWOL monitoring

### How the AWOL list works

- The list counts **Sundays missed in a row** since the member was last in church, not a total over a period. When the count reaches the number leadership set (3 by default), the member appears on the AWOL list by itself.
- **Any attendance resets the count to zero**, Sunday or midweek. Absences before their last visit never count.
- **Missing midweek never adds to the count.** Many members travel far and miss midweek for work, so only Sunday absence flags anyone.
- A Sunday nobody attended is skipped (treated as a service that did not hold), so it never counts against anyone.
- New members are counted from their registration date, so they are not flagged for Sundays before they joined.
- Someone away longer than the "Archive after" weeks (12 by default) is marked **Dormant**, so recent cases stand out.

Calling someone or writing a note does not take them off the list. A case closes in one of these ways:

- **They come back to church.** The case closes by itself and a system note is added to the record. Nothing for you to do.
- **Manage → Final status → Resolve.** Relocated or Attends another church takes them off the AWOL list for good. Active and Unknown close this case but keep them monitored: Unknown means hard to reach, not gone.

### Handling a case

1. Open the **AWOL Monitoring** card, then **My cases** or **AWOL list**. Type a name (first, last or both, in any order) or part of a phone number into the search box. It filters the view you are on and points you to any other view where they appear.
2. Tap **Manage** on the case. Use **Call** or **WhatsApp** to reach the member straight from the sheet.
3. Write what you found. Tick **Pastors only** on a note to hide it from everyone but pastors. Tap the **mic** to speak instead of typing (tap it again to stop; it works best in Chrome), then **Tidy** to turn it into a short note the next worker can follow. Check the tidied version and choose **Use this** or **Keep mine**: nothing is saved until you press **Save note** or **Resolve**.
4. Pick the **Final status**: Active (re-engaged), Unknown (cannot be reached, stays monitored), Relocated, or Attends another church (both leave the AWOL list for good).
5. Press **Resolve**. The case goes to the searchable **Archive** with the date and the full note trail.

### Who sees what

- **My cases** shows the cases assigned to you.
- Leaders **assign** cases to workers and **approve** a worker's request to handle one. Workers **request** a case they want to take.
- Leaders with the right access can press **Change AWOL list** to set how many Sundays in a row flag someone, when a case becomes Dormant, which services count, and which spiritual statuses are watched. Every change is kept in the rule history with its reason.

### The AWOL report

Press **AWOL report** on the Welfare tab. Choose a month or a date range. A confidential PDF downloads with the cases tracked and the rule that was in force.

## Welfare: birthdays and life events

- **Birthdays** lists the members (and Junior Church children) with birthdays this month and next.
- **Life events** lists weddings, dedications, births and other milestones. Press **Log event** to add one.
- Leaders press **Assign** to give a celebration to a Charis worker. The worker marks **Flyer posted** and then **Done**.
- **Monthly recap** (leaders) shows this month's celebrants. Tap a portrait to crop it or change the photo, then **Export** to save the celebration slides.

## Events: task-based logistics

Each event is planned as tasks, one per person. For a conference, Jane might get "Water and food" with a budget of ₦15,000 and Ruth "Fruits" with ₦8,000. Each person handles and reports only their task.

### Leaders: assigning tasks

1. On the **Events** tab, find the event and press **Tasks**.
2. Fill in **New task**: a clear title, a short description, the member, and an estimated budget. The member is told in the app at once.
3. Add as many tasks as the event needs. You see every task; members see only theirs.

### Members: reporting on your task

1. On the **Events** tab, find the event and press **Tasks**. You see only the tasks assigned to you.
2. When the money reaches you, press **Funds received**. It is recorded with the date.
3. After buying, press **Report**: what you bought, what you actually spent, and the receipt reference. The task is then marked completed.

### Signing off an event

When every task is done, the leader presses **Sign off** in the tasks sheet to apply their digital stamp. The Finance Director then presses **Countersign**. With both stamps on, **Report** downloads the sealed PDF.

**Sunday services** (on the same tab) creates every Sunday service for the month you pick, so tasks can be planned against them.

## Finance: budget, spending and forecast

1. **Set budget**: at the start of each month, enter the month, the year and the approved amount. It is the baseline for budget against spending.
2. **Log expense**: add every purchase with its category and amount, linked to an event when it belongs to one.
3. **Budget vs spent** shows the last six months; **By category** breaks down this month's spending.
4. **Next month** forecasts each category from the average of the last three months, which helps with next month's budget request.
5. Change the **Month** and **Year** at the top to look at any earlier month.

## Library: the book desk

1. **Add book**: fill in the title, author, category and format. Add the front cover (and the back cover), then press **Read cover** to fill in the title, author and description from the pictures. Add the e-book PDF when there is one.
2. **Borrowed books**: physical books members reserved or took. Press **Picked up** when a member collects a book, and **Return** when they bring it back.
3. **Waitlist**: when every copy is out, members can join the waitlist. They are told when a copy comes back.
4. **E-books** are read in the Member Portal, which keeps each reader's place.
